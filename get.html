the encrypted desktop app

Verbatim.

A word-perfect memory trainer that keeps everything encrypted on your own computer.

Paste a text, split it into blocks — verses, questions and answers, terms — and type each one from memory, checked to the letter. Tiny Verbatim in the browser practises the same way and forgets everything when you close it; Verbatim keeps what you learn, protected.

01 · What the app adds

  • Everything you keep is encrypted on your computer: entries, sessions, notes and tests, history, your personal dictionary and your settings (all but the theme and the screen language, which sit plainly beside the app so it can draw before you unlock). Files are padded, so their sizes say little about what is in them.
  • Accounts, each with a name and a PIN or password, and an admin side that manages them.
  • Secure collections behind passwords of their own — some can stay hidden behind a second password; an inspection of your files doesn't show whether any exist.
  • A library with folders, a 30-day Discarded shelf, and an encrypted backup you can check and restore.
  • Several vaults on one computer, each opened only by its own 12-word recovery phrase; nothing on the screen shows the others exist.
  • Protected share cards, encrypted, signed with the sender's own key and code name.

02 · Where Verbatim lives

Place Verbatim in a folder of your own choosing; its data sits beside it. It does not need to be Applications — Documents/Verbatim or a USB stick suits it better.

  • Verbatim keeps everything it stores in a folder beside itself, named after the app: Verbatim Data. Your vaults, encrypted, live there. Nothing goes to Application Support, and there are no accounts and no servers.
  • To back up, move or carry Verbatim, copy the app and that folder together.
  • Each app copy in its own folder is its own install, with its own vaults and its own theme and language.
  • Delete the data folder only if you mean to erase every vault in it: without a backup, nothing can bring them back.

macOS runs an app straight from a disk image or the Downloads folder from a hidden read-only place, so Verbatim opened there asks you to move it first.

04 · Opening an unsigned file

Mac

  1. Drag the app into a folder of yours and open it from there — never straight from the disk image.
  2. The first time, macOS says it cannot check the app: open System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Windows

  1. Windows SmartScreen stops a file it doesn't know: choose More info, then Run anyway.

Linux

  1. An AppImage is made executable and run: chmod +x Verbatim.AppImage. The .deb installs with your package manager.

05 · Cards, in plain words

A Tiny Verbatim card is a container, not a hiding place: it is encoded, not encrypted, and anyone holding it can read what it says and who sent it. The picture openly shows its title, an emoji and a short identifier.

A Verbatim card is encrypted; its code travels another way, and only the sender's mark and code name show. Every card one sender signs carries the same code name, so they can be linked — the point of a signature, and its cost.

06 · What it sends

  • Verbatim sends nothing on its own. Every send is started by you, and the screen shows what goes and where: a share card, an export, a feedback report, courier sync, a check for updates.
  • Everything you keep is encrypted on your computer: entries, sessions, notes and tests, history, your personal dictionary and your settings (all but the theme and the screen language, which sit plainly beside the app so it can draw before you unlock). Files are padded, so their sizes say little about what is in them.
  • Some collections can stay hidden behind a second password. An inspection of your files doesn't show whether any hidden collections exist; copies compared over time can show that something changed, never what changed.
  • One key hides and locks. The panic shortcut blanks the screen, locks, and clears anything Verbatim put on the clipboard. Work you have not saved is kept in a protected part of your library, so you can pick it up after a crash. Deleting everything is possible: in Settings, after unlocking, by typing DELETE and confirming.
